## A Game Defined by Co-op Gameplay
Borderlands is at the cutting edge of co-op gameplay, constantly creating new systems to make it easier and more fun to play with your friends. A big part of that is SHiFT, the online subsystem that fuels Gearbox games, with friends, entitlements, and more all wrapped inside.

## My Contributions

* Complete overhaul of the SHiFT menu, including a rework of navigation, the friends page, and removal of the news page
* Updates across the SHiFT platform to meet COPPA requirements
* Creation of a new micro-patching system
* Improved cross-play support for pc and current generation consoles
* Cross-progression support for pc and current generation consoles
