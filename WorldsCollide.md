## Dedication to the Craft

Worlds Collide was a game jam game I created in 48 hours as a solo team in a competition primarily intended for groups of 4 or more. It was created in GameMaker engine, and contains almost a dozen micro-games. Although it didn't win, I did receive good feedback and praise for the amount of work completed solo in the time provided.
